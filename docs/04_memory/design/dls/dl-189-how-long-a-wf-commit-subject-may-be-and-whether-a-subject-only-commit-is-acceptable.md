## Context

The release-health report of v0.3 shows two commit-message metrics regressing: G02 (share of commits with a body) fell three runs in a row (43.86 · 41.36 · 37.81), and G04 (subject length) rose (mean 99.71 → 115.9, share over 72 characters 77.63 → 87.07). `wf()` commits are 69.67 % of the window (G13), most have no body, and their subjects carry full slugged ids (`git log -1 --format=%s a6089acd | wc -c` → 166).

## Decision

Options, for the approver:

- **(a)** Accept subject-only `wf()` commits (the subject plus trailers is the record) and exclude them from G02; bound the `{slug}` token of `id_pattern` (for example 40 characters) so subjects shorten for new elements.
- **(b)** Keep G02 as is and let `wf()` subjects name the short id `<prefix>-<n>` instead of the full id.
- **(c)** Change nothing in the format; amend the catalogue's floors for the two metrics.

Recommendation: (a): ids stay immutable for existing elements, the format grammar is unchanged, and the metrics measure what they were meant to.

## Rationale

A metric that regresses because of the governed tool's own output is measuring the tool, not the practice; ids are part of every reference, so a short-id subject (b) would make the subject and the id diverge.

## Actions

- At v0.4 planning: rule an option; (a) or (b) become a task (`id_pattern` / commit-message), (c) a catalogue amendment (`dl-173`).
