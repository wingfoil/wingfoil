## Context

**Only a transition produces a readable approval record** (operation, `Approver:`, `Reason:`, P1.7, P1.10). Two
consequences reach a consumer:

1. **The approval that closes a phase is not in the plan's history.** A phase declared with `approval: { by_role:
   approver }` is recorded by `workflow finalize --reason …` as a `workflow: finalize <instance> <step>` commit with
   `Approver:` and `Reason:` and the `WingFoil-*` trailers (AGENTS.md §5.1); `workflow end` then writes
   `wf(plan): finalize <instance> [active → done]` with neither line. In a scratch Kanban repository on 0.3.2,
   `wingfoil memory history sw-life-cycle-1-plan --format json` → entries `add` and `submit` only, `approver: null`;
   the finalize record of the approval step is not listed (`memory history` does not read it as a Memory operation).
   Without the engine, the scaffolded plan (`waiting: [active]`) cannot be closed by any verb: `wingfoil workflow end`
   before sunset → `is not complete: current step sw-life-cycle.sunset`, and `submit`/`approve` refuse a waiting
   state.
2. **A decision that moves nothing has no record at all.** A spending consent, an ordering of work, a scope ruling:
   the approver can only write prose in an ordinary commit, with no `Approver:` or `Reason:` a tool reads. Not
   reproduced (a design request); `grep -rliE "decision without a transition|moves no element" docs/04_memory` finds
   no element.

## Decision

Options:
- **(a) One verb for a decision without a transition, and history that reads phase records.** A new Memory operation
  `memory decide <id> --reason "…"` (approver role, authority checked as for `approve`) commits
  `wf({type}): decide {id} [s → s]` with `Approver:` and `Reason:` and no content change, added to the closed verb list
  (`dl-079`, `MEMORY_OPERATIONS`). `memory history <plan>` also lists the `workflow:` records whose
  `WingFoil-Instance:` is that plan, with their approver and reason, marked as phase records.
- **(b) Extend existing verbs.** `memory approve <id> --decision "…"` leaves the state as it is; `workflow end` copies
  the last approval phase's `Approver:` and `Reason:` into the plan's finalize commit.
- **(c) Decisions are elements.** A decision that moves nothing is a `decision-log` taken to `ready`; history is
  unchanged.

**Recommendation: (a).** It gives each record one meaning: `approve` always moves a state, `decide` never does, and a
plan's audit trail includes the approvals of its phases without copying them into a second commit. (b) overloads
`approve` with a mode in which it does not approve a transition and duplicates the approval record at `end`; (c) is
heavy for a one-line consent and does not answer the plan-history half.

## Rationale

- **P1.10 promises the audit trail of an element**; for a plan, the approvals of its phases are part of that trail.
- **A closed verb list stays closed** by adding one explicit verb with its bracket rule, rather than giving an
  existing verb a second meaning.
