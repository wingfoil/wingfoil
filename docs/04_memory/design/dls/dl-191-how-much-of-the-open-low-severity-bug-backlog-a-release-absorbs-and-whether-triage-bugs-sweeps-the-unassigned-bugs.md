## Context

Q20 (bug net flow) is −50 over v0.3: 263 bugs added, 213 reached `closed` or `deprecated`; Q13 adds 131 open, 113 of them low, 18 with no release. The v0.3 retrospective triaged the 18 by hand (plan ruling D5).

## Decision

Options:

- **(a)** `release-planning.triage-bugs` sweeps every `open` bug and every `triaged` bug with no `release`, so none stays unassigned past a planning; a release reserves a share of its budget (for example 10 %) for low-severity bugs, oldest first.
- **(b)** Only (a)'s sweep, no reservation.
- **(c)** No rule; the catalogue's Q20 floor is amended to a trend.

Recommendation: (a).

## Rationale

A net flow that stays negative turns the low-severity backlog into permanent debt; a sweep at planning is the one point where every bug is seen.

- At v0.4 planning: rule; (a) amends `release-planning.yaml` (`triage-bugs`, `define-scope`).
