## Decision

The question: is Casey a persona who approves and rejects (and deprecates) Memory elements, or only a reader of the
project's state? Options:

- **(A) Casey only reads.** `dl-113` Q2 (a) holds as written. Journey 5 step 8 becomes "Casey sees what awaits a
  decision and who holds it" (read-only, `workflow status` / `memory search`), the *Success* line drops
  "stakeholder approvals"; Casey leaves the persona column of P1.7, P1.8, P1.9, P4.14 and P4.15; US-5-10 is
  rewritten as a read story or removed; P4.14's `by_person: Casey` scenario names another person, and the X1.2
  "budget" scenario routes an information notification, not a decision.
- **(B) Casey approves when holding the `approver` role.** Approval is a role, not a persona (REQ-SYS-08); a
  stakeholder who holds `approver` for some decisions (budget, scope) approves through the same verbs. The persona
  and the brief are corrected from "never changes it" to "changes state only through an approval the DNA assigns
  to them", and `dl-113` Q2 (a) is read as "Casey's *own* goals are views". Journey 5, US-5-10, P4.14 and X1.2 stay.
- **(C) Casey approves only through a non-CLI surface** (a later user interface, `dl-008-cli-first-no-gui`), so the
  journey step stays but leaves the MVP, and the CLI stories move to Morgan.

**Recommendation: (B).** It matches how WingFoil already works — authority is a role bound in `dna.yaml`, and the
journey's own success criterion ("stakeholder approvals happen in parallel") is a real use; (A) would remove a
use the P4.14 and X1.2 BDD files and the feature list all assume, and (C) defers a capability that already ships. The
correction is then one sentence each in `04_personas.md` and `01_product-brief.md`, plus a note on `dl-113`.

## Rationale

- **Role over persona.** REQ-SYS-08 binds directives and approvals to roles, never to people; a persona is a
  description of needs, not an authority. "Never changes state" describes Casey's goals, not what the system lets
  an `approver` do.
- **Fewest documents move under (B):** two sentences, against five layers under (A).
- **Trade-off of (B):** Casey is no longer the pure read-only persona `dl-113` drew, and the read-only views remain
  the larger part of Casey's goals; the persona must say both.
