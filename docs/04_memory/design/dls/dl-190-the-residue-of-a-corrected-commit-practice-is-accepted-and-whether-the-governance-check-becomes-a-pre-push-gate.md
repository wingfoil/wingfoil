## Decision

1. The residue of a corrected practice, dated before the correction, is **accepted** and recorded as such (as `bug-401`); release-health reads it as residue, not regression.
2. Options for the gate:
   - **(a)** make the governance check a pre-push gate the `git-conventions` directive requires (the hook installed by the contributor setup), so a malformed `wf()` commit is refused before it is pushed;

   - **(c)** both (a) and a release-publishing step that waits for the required check before the tag.

Recommendation: 1 and (c).
