## Description

Feature **P5.3.1** (`agent execute`). `dl-181` (`ready`, `c74c7ae30`) splits `spec-016` §3.5's headless launch into a
**work run** (v0.4: an agent works without a terminal, no verdict, outcome = its commits and `exit_status`) and the
v1.0 verification run, with Q1 (a) `result: n/a`, Q3 (c) the prompt channel per adapter and Q4 (a) `--timeout`.
`dl-183` (`ready`, `64e22ac60`) decides who chooses the kind: Q1 (a) a phase field `launch: interactive | headless |
any` defaulting to `interactive`; Q2 (a) the option over the field with a warning on a contradiction; Q3 (a) a
refusal when the adapter lacks the kind; Q4 (a) `--next` resolves the step first. `dl-171` (c): `--next` refuses a
step that only waits. Prerequisite of the print-mode pilot (`release-planning-rel-v0.4-plan` R1 L8).
`bug-392`: `--dry-run` with a built-in adapter refuses without a terminal although it launches nothing. `bug-380`:
`spec-016` and `spec-017` cite `X_cli-cmds.md` by bare line numbers that moved.

## Acceptance Criteria

1. (red-first) A phase's `launch:` key (`interactive | headless | any`, default `interactive`) gives the default kind;
   an unknown value is a load diagnostic (`spec-003` Layer 2 phase table and § Diagnostics, amendment `spec-003` Layer 2 Phase table and § Diagnostics `launch`;
   `dl-183` Q1 (a)).
2. (red-first) `--headless` / `--interactive` override the phase field; an override that contradicts a declared kind
   prints `dl-183` Q2's warning; both options together exit `2`; an approver-role step is never launched
   (`dl-183` Q2 (a), Decision; `spec-008` §12, amendment `spec-008` § 10 `--set`, § 12 command flags).
3. (red-first) An adapter that declares no headless launch is refused before any side effect, `VALIDATION`, exit `1`,
   with the new `spec-016` §3.7 row; under `any` the declared kind is used, `interactive` first (`dl-183` Q3 (a)).
4. (red-first) A headless work run launches with no terminal, reads `launch.headless.*` and `prompt.via` from the
   adapter (`dl-181` Q3 (c)), forwards nothing to a terminal it does not have, and is recorded with `result: n/a`
   (`spec-016` §3.5 split into the work run and the v1.0 verification run, amendment `spec-016` § 3.1 grammar, § 3.2 resolution, § 3.3 pipeline, § 3.5 headless, § 3.7 refusals; §2.2, amendment `spec-016` § 2.2 schema, § 2.8 built-ins
   headless).
5. (red-first) `--timeout <duration>` sends `SIGTERM`, then `SIGKILL` after the grace period; the record is still
   written with `exit_status: "signal:SIGTERM"` (or `"signal:SIGKILL"`) and `agent execute` exits `1` with
   `agent timed out after <duration>; run <run-id> recorded` (`dl-181` Q4 (a); `spec-008` § 10 `--set`, § 12 command flags).
6. (red-first) `agent execute --next` refuses a frontier step that waits only for an approval or a record, then applies
   the launch-kind rule to the step it resolves (`dl-171` (c), `dl-183` Q4 (a); new `spec-016` §3.7 row, `spec-016` § 3.1 grammar, § 3.2 resolution, § 3.3 pipeline, § 3.5 headless, § 3.7 refusals).
7. (red-first) `--dry-run` with a built-in adapter succeeds without a terminal and launches nothing (`bug-392`).
8. (red-first, checked by command) `spec-016` and `spec-017` cite `X_cli-cmds.md` by section heading, not by line:
   `grep -nE "X_cli-cmds\.md:[0-9]" docs/04_memory/design/specs/spec-01[67]-*.md` prints nothing after the change and
   prints the stale citations before it (`bug-380`; `claim-evidence`).
9. (characterization) The built-in adapters that gain `launch.headless` are verified by hand with their
   `verified_with` (`dl-181` Q2 (a) rationale; `spec-016` §2.8), recorded in the Execution Notes with the CLI version
   read.
10. (characterization) The spec amendments `spec-003` Layer 2 Phase table and § Diagnostics (`launch`), `spec-008` § 10 `--set`, § 12 command flags (`--headless`, `--interactive`, `--timeout`), `spec-016` § 2.2 schema, § 2.8 built-ins
    (headless) and `spec-016` § 3.1 grammar, § 3.2 resolution, § 3.3 pipeline, § 3.5 headless, § 3.7 refusals are approved at the design gate; the design checks the `launch:` key against the pinned build
    (`dl-205` Q1 (a)).
