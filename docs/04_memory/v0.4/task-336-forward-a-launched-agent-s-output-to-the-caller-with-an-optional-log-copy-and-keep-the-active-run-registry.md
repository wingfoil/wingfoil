## Description

Feature **P5.3.1**. `dl-182` (`ready`, `55dd9498a`, Q1–Q5 (a)): a headless run's output is forwarded line by line to
the caller's stderr and parsed from the same stream, `--log <path>` appends a copy and warns on a tracked or
non-ignored path, WingFoil keeps no log of its own, interactive runs stay unchanged with `--log` refused, and the
documentation states that output may hold secrets because it never reaches the committed record. `dl-181` ships the
work run with `spec-016` §7's active registry (a headless run has no terminal to show it is alive).

## Acceptance Criteria

1. (red-first) In a headless run the agent's stdout and stderr reach the caller's stderr line by line; `agent
   execute`'s stdout stays the `{ "run": <record> }` payload (`dl-182` Q1 (a); `spec-005` §2; `spec-016` §3.3,
   amendment `spec-016` § 3.1 grammar, § 3.2 resolution, § 3.3 pipeline, § 3.5 headless, § 3.7 refusals).
2. (red-first) `--log <path>` appends a copy, creates the file if absent, and warns when `git check-ignore` says the
   path is tracked or not ignored; on an interactive launch `--log` exits `2` (`dl-182` Q2 (a), Q4 (a); `spec-008` §12,
   amendment `spec-008` § 10 `--set`, § 12 command flags `--log`).
3. (red-first) Nothing of the output is written to the repository or the run record, and WingFoil keeps no log of its
   own (`dl-182` Q3 (a)).
4. (red-first) A running launch is listed in the active registry under `.wingfoil/run/` and removed when it exits,
   stale entries pruned on read (`spec-016` §7, amendment `spec-016` § 7, § 8 registry half).
5. (red-first) `.wingfoil/run/` is the one git-ignored subpath `wingfoil init` writes (`spec-011` § `.gitignore`
   policy, amendment `spec-011` § `.gitignore` policy).
6. (red-first) `agent list` reports the running launches it reads from the registry (`spec-016` §7).
7. (red-first) The user guide's launch section and the CLI reference state that an agent's output may hold secrets and
   is never committed (`dl-182` Q5 (a)).
8. (characterization) The spec amendments `spec-008` § 10 `--set`, § 12 command flags (`--log`), `spec-011` § `.gitignore` policy, `spec-016` § 3.1 grammar, § 3.2 resolution, § 3.3 pipeline, § 3.5 headless, § 3.7 refusals (output) and `spec-016` § 7, § 8 (registry; the MCP rows stay w1)
   are approved at the design gate.
