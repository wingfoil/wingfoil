# spec-016 and fake.yaml say the fake adapter declares every §2.2 field, but it declares no mcp.conflict_args

## Summary

`spec-016-agent-execution` §2.7 says the fake adapter "covers every §2.2 field", and the header of
`test/fixtures/agents/custom/fake.yaml` says it "declares every §2.2 field". `mcp.conflict_args` is a §2.2
field (added by `task-356`), and none of the three fixture manifests (`fake.yaml`, `fake-terminal.yaml`,
`fake-session-output.yaml`) declares it.

## Steps to Reproduce

1. `grep -n 'mcp.conflict_args' docs/04_memory/design/specs/spec-016-agent-execution.md` — a row of the §2.2
   table.
2. `grep -c conflict_args test/fixtures/agents/custom/*.yaml` — `0` for each of the three files.
3. `sed -n 1,3p test/fixtures/agents/custom/fake.yaml` and `sed -n 344,350p` of the spec — the claim.

## Expected Behavior

Either the fake adapter declares every §2.2 field, as both texts say, or both texts name the fields it leaves
out.

## Actual Behavior

The claim is false for `mcp.conflict_args`; the conflict check is tested with manifests built inside the tests.

## Notes

Found by the architect at `task-339`'s design; `task-339` adds a further §2.2 field
(`launch.<mode>.permissions`) and, by its ruling D10, leaves `fake.yaml` unchanged, so the claim falls further
behind. Fix: reword the two texts ("every §2.2 field except …"), or declare the missing fields in `fake.yaml` if
no test depends on their absence.
