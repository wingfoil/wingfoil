## Summary

`COLLABORATION.md` tells a consumer project to ask for its feedback inbox to be registered through the **Proposal**
issue form, but that form says it is captured as a `decision-log` element, while a registration is a `service` of kind
`repository` recorded through `service-ingest`; nothing tells the maintainer who reads the issue which workflow to run.

## Steps to Reproduce

1. `sed -n 186,189p COLLABORATION.md` → "**Asking to be registered.** A project asks through the **Proposal** issue
   form (*How to contribute*, above), naming its repository and its inbox."
2. `sed -n 180,185p COLLABORATION.md` → the registration itself is "an `active` `service` of kind `repository` whose
   `feedback_inbox` names that folder", recorded "through the `service-ingest` workflow".
3. `sed -n 5p .github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/proposal.yml` → `description: Propose a change to WingFoil's product or process.
   It is captured as a \`decision-log\` element.`; `sed -n 1,3p .github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/proposal.yml` → the form is
   "shaped on `decision-log-ingest`".
4. `grep -n -i 'regist\|inbox\|service' .github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE/proposal.yml; echo $?` → `1`: the form says nothing about
   a registration request. `grep -n -i 'issue\|proposal' .wingfoil/workflows/custom/service-ingest.yaml; echo $?` → `1`:
   `service-ingest`'s `capture` says nothing about an issue as its source.

## Expected Behavior

A registration request filed through the Proposal form is routed to `service-ingest`, not captured as a
`decision-log`: either the form says so (one line in its description or its first markdown block: "a request to
register a consumer feedback inbox is recorded as a `service` through `service-ingest`"), or `service-ingest`'s
`capture` names a Proposal issue as a source, or `COLLABORATION.md` names a different channel.

## Actual Behavior

The form and `COLLABORATION.md` disagree on what a registration request becomes; a maintainer following the form
literally runs `decision-log-ingest` and records a decision-log for what is a `service`.
