## Summary
`task-328` (w2 of `patch-v0.3.2`) adds two gated checks to `scripts/check-governance.cjs`: `trailer-paragraph` (a `WingFoil-*` line
outside a commit's final paragraph, reported under the `body` rule, on every non-merge commit) and `workflow-record-authority` (an
`Approver:` line on a `workflow: finalize|adopt|rekey` record, reported under `body` and `authority`). `scripts/release-health/measure.cjs`
builds P01 from `groupFindings(governance, ['subject', 'bracket', 'body'])` over the denominator `governance.checked` (the `wf(...)` commits
only, line 1010), and P05 from `groupFindings(governance, ['authority'])` (line 968). So a `trailer-paragraph` finding on a `docs:`/`feat:`
commit or a record, and a record's `body` finding, now count in P01, whose definition (`docs/08_health/metrics.yaml`) is "`wf(...)` commits
whose subject and body are well-formed"; a record's `authority` finding counts in P05. The numerator then mixes commits the denominator
does not hold.

## Steps to Reproduce
1. After `task-328`'s merge, make a non-`wf` commit with a `WingFoil-Instance:` line outside its final paragraph on a release's window.
2. Run release-health `measure` for that release: P01's failing list names the commit, though it is no `wf(...)` commit.
(Found by reading the code; the new checks have 0 findings over all history today, so no published figure changed — `task-328`'s review,
whole-history run.)

## Expected Behavior
P01 counts only the findings on `wf(...)` commits (or the metric is redefined to the checks it should cover), and P05 only those on
approvals and run records, each consistent with its denominator and its catalogue definition; a test pins it.

## Actual Behavior
P01 and P05 group findings by rule only, so findings of the new checks on other commits enter them.
