## Summary

Some checks in this repository's workflow configuration are still prose, or are explained wrongly, even though a
command or a short change could settle them. Four items were found during `task-303`'s design and review. The fix is
one tidy-up of `.wingfoil/workflows/custom/*.yaml` and `.wingfoil/workflows/bindings.yaml`:

1. `e2e-smoke.mcp-registration` repeats as prose what its own action already asserts.
2. The `agent-docs` checks on AGENTS.md and CLAUDE.md have no command that could back them.
3. A comment in `release-health.yaml` gives a reason that no longer holds.
4. The reason given for `proposals.disposed` explains the wrong thing, and three checks mix a part a command can
   decide with a part only a person can judge.

## Expected Behavior

- `e2e-smoke.mcp-registration` declares `mcp.registered` as its post-check in place of the two prose checks (and the
  `exit-code-zero` check that `check:mcp` already covers). Its description still says what the script asserts.
- An AGENTS.md parity suite under `test/docs/`, like `workflow-md.test.ts`, gives the `agent-docs` checks a `run`
  binding, or the checks say why they stay a reading.
- The `release-health.yaml` comment states the real reason the check is unbound, or the check gets a command.
- The reason on `proposals.disposed` says why completeness cannot be decided by a command. Each mixed check is split
  into a part a command can decide, left unbound until a command exists so the gap stays visible, and a
  `manual: true` judgement.

`manual: true` (so their mechanical part is hidden) or fully unbound.
